# Business Model

UVote is freemium and two-sided.

### Citizens (B2C)

The free tier is fully functional: voting on bills, plain-language summaries, and all three alignment scores (Constituent, District, and Representation). Premium subscription (~$5/mo or ~$40/yr) unlocks historical voting trends, cross-district comparison, custom notifications, and deeper engagement analytics. Premium adds depth, never access.

### Elected Officials (B2B)

Official Office tier ($199 to $499/mo depending on district size) provides a verified profile, a full constituent analytics dashboard, and the tools to respond directly when their Constituent score shows a divergence: publishing their explanation for a mismatched vote directly to the district that's watching it. This tier is where unit economics become compelling, replacing a fragmented mix of newsletters, town halls, and social monitoring with one structured constituent intelligence platform.

### The Two-Sided Flywheel

The two sides reinforce each other: an official with a public, verified presence and a visible alignment score gives their district a reason to engage on UVote, and citizen density in a district is what makes an official's subscription valuable in the first place. This two-sided seeding loop is the core growth engine in every new market.

### Future Exploration (not committed or scoped)

A third revenue path is under long-term consideration: the same concentration of likely voters that gives UVote a structural moat (see `scaling-and-growth.md`) could eventually be made available to third parties as a paid research or survey panel, with users potentially compensated directly for participation. This is contingent on reaching meaningful scale and is not part of the current business model.
